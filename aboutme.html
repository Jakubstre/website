→ About me

Jakub Hudaček

I am a Slovak singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, composer and producer working across music, theatre, design and digital media. My work is built on atmosphere, emotional detail and complete creative control from idea to final output.

→ Music

Music is the core of my work. I write, perform, produce and engineer my own material, both for Midheaven and for solo and theatre projects. My sound tends toward emotional tension, quiet intensity and slow development rather than spectacle. I am interested in songs that feel lived-in, honest and precise.

→ Midheaven

Midheaven is my main band and long-term creative outlet. Through it, I explore distance, memory, transition, home, and the feeling of carrying things that are not easy to say directly. The project combines songwriting, arrangement, production and visual identity into one larger world.

→ Theatre

I also compose for theatre, where music has to support story, timing and atmosphere. That work taught me how to write with restraint and how to let sound carry emotion without taking over the scene. I enjoy projects where music needs to function as part of a larger dramatic structure.

→ Design

Alongside music, I work in graphic design and web design. I like building clear visual systems that feel personal rather than generic. Whether I am designing a poster, a cover, a website or a campaign, I aim for consistency, readability and a strong identity.

→ Approach

I prefer work that is carefully made and emotionally coherent. I care about the relationship between sound, image and meaning, and I like projects that feel complete rather than fragmented. I usually work best when I can shape both the artistic and technical sides of something from the beginning.

→ Now

At the moment I am continuing to develop Midheaven, building theatre work, and creating visual material for my own projects and collaborations. I am always open to meaningful commissions, especially where music and design meet.
